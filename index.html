Our Services

Discover Our Expertise — all 7 services

1. Terrace & Roof

Most common Bangalore monsoon problem. Clean, repair cracks, strengthen weak areas, apply coatings/membranes.

2. Bathroom & Toilet

Continuous water exposure. Durable chemical coats + joint sealing. Moisture-free, no paint peeling below.

3. External Wall

Rain + drainage issues. Fill gaps, apply sun/rain-proof coatings. Improves life & appearance.

4. Basement & Foundation

Soil moisture, fungus, structural weakness. Membrane + pressure-resistant coatings. Dry & safe basement.

5. Overhead & Underground Tank

Food-grade safe materials. Stops cracks & seepage, clean stored water, longer tank life.

Seals gaps, fixes slope, protects joints/edges. Ideal for apartments with wind-driven rain.

7. Crack Filling & Leakage Repair

Polyurethane, epoxy, polymer mortar. Permanent seal for walls/ceilings/slabs.
