About Us

Bangalore's trusted waterproofing team

Omkar Waterproofing Services is one of Bangalore's most trusted names in professional waterproofing solutions, known for long-lasting protection against leakage, seepage and moisture damage. We serve homeowners, commercial establishments, builders and industries across the city.

We started with a simple belief: every building deserves to stay strong, dry and safe. Water damage is the biggest enemy of any structure — our purpose is to eliminate it with right technologies, materials and expert workmanship.

Based in Bangalore, we understand heavy monsoons, flat terrace structures and ageing construction. Our solutions are designed for the region's harsh weather.

Our team has worked on countless homes, apartments, independent houses, villas and commercial buildings — from small cracks to large-scale seepage.

Waterproofing is an investment, not just repair. A small leak today becomes major structural damage tomorrow. We diagnose accurately: inspect site, find root cause, choose most effective method.

Materials: PU coatings, acrylic polymers, cementitious coatings, membranes, high-grade sealants and crack fillers — selected per surface & environment. Careful preparation, cleaning, priming and layering with proper drying — never rushed.

Transparent & honest: clear issue explanation, work plan, no hidden charges. We simplify waterproofing so you feel confident. After-service support, maintenance guidance and warranty included.

Neat, minimal disturbance, on-time completion. From household repairs to large commercial projects — once waterproofed by us, no more leakage worry.
